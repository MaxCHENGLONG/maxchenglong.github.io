## Introduction

This post surveys the architectures of representative open-weight LLMs as of October 2026, grouped by origin:

- **Chinese labs**: DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Kimi
- **US/European labs**: Llama, Mistral, gpt-oss, Gemma, Grok

Closed frontier models such as GPT, Gemini, and Claude are not covered in detail, since their parameter counts and architectures are not publicly disclosed.
